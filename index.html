Teacher's Pedagogical Guide

Lesson Target: English File Elementary 4B (Daily Routine, Present Simple 3rd person singular -s/-es, Time Prepositions at/in/from...to).

  • Activity 1 (Chrono-Sorter): Active schemata activation. Students process lexical chunks (verb phrases) and logically reconstruct the chronology of the day, decoupling grammar anxiety from semantic sequencing.
  • Activity 2 (Sentence Builder): Controlled productive transformation. Moves students from the non-finite base verb phrase ("make breakfast") to third-person singular syntax ("After yoga, she makes breakfast.").
  • Activity 3 (Scaffolded Cloze): Three distinct cognitive tiers:
    • Level 1: Lexical verb agreement (verbs only: wakes, makes, drinks).
    • Level 2: Phrasal verbs and collocations (wakes up, makes breakfast).
    • Level 3: Functional discourse & time prepositions (at, from, after).
